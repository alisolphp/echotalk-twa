EchoTalk is a privacy-first, offline shadowing tool that helps you master pronunciation and fluency through deliberate practice.

✨ Highlights:
• Works fully offline after the first load – no internet required.
• Zero data collection – recordings and progress stay only on your device.
• Free & Open Source (MIT) – the web version is available as a PWA on GitHub.
• Multi-language support (15 languages): English, German, French, and more.
• Record your voice and compare it against the model pronunciation.
• Smart training modes: Auto-Skip, Auto-Repetition, Check mode, and adaptive speed.
• Optional AI analysis for pronunciation, grammar, and creative exercises.
• Practice streak calendar with motivational messages to keep you going.

📦 This app is completely free, and has no annoying ads.
